Whold'em rules: Four-player No-Limit Hold'em-style play with $10/$20 blinds and $1,000 starting stacks. You receive two private cards and share five community cards. Betting occurs pre-flop, on the flop, turn, and river. On your turn, tap the $10/$20/$50/$100 chips as many times as you like to build a bet or raise, then press Bet/Raise. The best five-card poker hand wins. Tied hands split the pot. Side pots are handled for all-ins. Computer players use simple hand-strength and pot-pressure logic rather than seeing your cards. Chip sounds can be toggled with the Sound button. Your player name is saved in this browser.
